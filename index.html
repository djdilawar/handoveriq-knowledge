Section 2 Mandatory acceptance requirements
REQ-01 Commissioning evidence: All tests T-01 through T-08 for the current scope must pass in an approved commissioning report. Any failed test keeps this requirement unmet until an approved retest report supersedes it. Accountable owner: Casey Morgan, Commissioning Lead.
REQ-02 Shift training evidence: Named representatives of shifts A, B and C must complete the handover training and have signed attendance verified by the Training Lead. A booking or verbal update is insufficient. Accountable owner: Riley Grant, Training Lead.
REQ-03 Current runbook approval: Runbook NS-B-RB-001 revision 2 must have written Operations approval for the expanded scope. Revision 1 covers the earlier configuration and is insufficient for this handover. Accountable owner: Sam Rivera, Documentation Lead. Required approver: Jordan Ellis.
REQ-04 Asset inventory: The final inventory for racks R-B01 through R-B06 must be verified by the Asset Lead. Accountable owner: Taylor Brooks, Asset Lead.
REQ-05 Support coverage: Vendor coverage for the expanded equipment must be active on the target handover date with a recorded escalation route. Accountable owner: Devon Lee, Vendor Manager.
REQ-06 Monitoring acceptance: The agreed 24-hour monitoring acceptance window must be completed, with the Monitoring Lead's signed acceptance. This requirement validates monitoring coverage. It does not substitute for commissioning tests. Accountable owner: Jamie Quinn, Monitoring Lead.

Section 3 Readiness decision rule
All six requirements are mandatory. Recommend Ready for owner acceptance only when all six are Supported. Otherwise recommend Hold pending evidence or closure and identify the affected requirements. There are no approved waivers in this baseline pack. A meeting summary or dashboard color does not waive a requirement. The agent's recommendation is advisory. Jordan Ellis makes the final handover decision.

Section 4 Evidence control
Use the document register NS-B-REG-001 to establish current approved records. Drafts may explain a planned correction but do not prove an approved result. Approval status describes acceptance of a record as accurate; an approved report may accurately record a failed test. Retain that distinction.
Supplemental evidence can be considered when its project scope, verifier and effective time are stated. Apply later evidence only at an appropriate assessment cutoff and identify the change from the baseline.
